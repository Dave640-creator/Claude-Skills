Regular Expressions — Quick Quiz

Answers
Part I — Create the Regex
1. 4-digit PIN — ^[0-9]{4}$
2. "cat" or "dog" — ^(cat|dog)$
3. One or more lowercase letters — ^[a-z]+$
4. Phone ###-#### — ^[0-9]{3}-[0-9]{4}$
5. "yes" any case — ^[Yy][Ee][Ss]$
Part II — Find the Matches
6. Regex given: a*
Matches: "" (empty string), aaa    Rejects: b
7. Regex given: ^[0-9]{2}$
Matches: 42, 07    Rejects: 123
8. Regex given: ^[aeiou]$
Matches: a, e    Rejects: b
9. Regex given: ^Hello
Matches: Hello, Hello world!    Rejects: Say Hello
10. Regex given: colou?r
Matches: color, colour    Rejects: colouur
